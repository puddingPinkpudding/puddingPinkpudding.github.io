🍮 【プジャ】 PUDYA (LORE)

Pudya 🍮

Universe: Pudding Cooked to New Year's Music

The Flawless Doll: Pudya appears completely lifeless, ignores almost everyone, and acts far too "perfect" in front of adults. She has no virtues; she has no flaws. She is a total blank slate on the outside.

Inside Her Mind: However, if you could peer into her head, you would find that Pudya simply doesn't know how to communicate. No one ever taught her. While Locke's solution granted her massive intellectual brainpower, mental and emotional intelligence require someone to guide her—and she has no one.

Her Hidden Dream: She does have dreams. She secretly wishes to be an ordinary living pudding, freed from her stupid, boring, expressionless face.

🍒 【チェリー】 CHERRY (LORE)

Cherry 🍒

Universe: Pudding Cooked to New Year's Music

The Golden Child: Cherry is the youngest child in his family, meaning he usually gets the leftovers. His parents send him allowance for food, but he chooses to starve himself just to buy absurdly expensive gifts for Pudya.

The Misunderstanding: He constantly tries to analyze Pudya's cold attitude using anime archetypes like "tsundere" or "kuudere", completely missing the fact that she just lacks communication skills. He takes her bluntness as a challenge.

🥟 【ユツコ】 YUTSUKO (LORE)

Yutsuko 🥟

Universe: Grocery Store Workers

The Only Guy: A 20-year-old janitor who is a Gyoza dumpling. He is the only male worker among the store staff (besides Director Kim). He loves cracking jokes, likes spicy and sour foods, but absolutely despises sweets.

🍙 【ミサクラ】 MISAKURA (LORE)

Misakura 🍙

Universe: Grocery Store Workers

The Rebel Onigiri: A 19-year-old junior security guard who happens to be a rice ball. Ironically, she hates seafood and shrimp, even though she is stuffed with them. Instead of dyeing her hair, she pours sweet chili or cheese sauces over herself. She refuses to wear skirts.

🥓 【キム】 KIM (LORE)

Kim 🥓

Universe: Grocery Store Workers

The Mysterious Boss: The 32-year-old strict manager of the shop. He is made of soy meat, though everyone constantly mistakes him for bacon. He holds hidden connections with a secret lab that studies abnormal citizens, and rumor has it he mutated due to radiation.

🐟 【アイコ】 AIKO (LORE)

Aiko 🐟

Universe: Grocery Store Workers

Burned Out: A 27-year-old senior cashier who is a Taiyaki fish pastry. She is completely burned out after 6 years of working here, staying only because of the decent salary. She is highly insecure about her short height, loves skirts, and aggressively envies Pudya 2's income.

🍡 【ユメニ】 YUMENI (LORE)

Yumeni 🍡

Universe: Grocery Store Workers

Here for the Vibe: A trainee cashier who is a Dango stick. She is very weird: despite getting her salary cut and extra shifts piled on her, she refuses to quit because she "likes the supermarket vibe". She loves dangerously spicy food and sniffing the grocery store air.

🍮 【プジャ】 PUDYA 2 (LORE)

Pudya 2 🍮

Universe: Grocery Store Workers

The Warehouse Queen: The 25-year-old warehouse supervisor. She earns the highest salary in the shop. She is incredibly strong, easily lifting 5kg crates, giving her a chubby and powerful build. She loves utilizing her job perk: eating food items that are just about to expire.
